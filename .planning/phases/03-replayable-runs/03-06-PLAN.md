# Phase 3 Plan 06: incurred versus notional cost, and the cache hit rate in summaries

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** replay eval reruns from a request-hash cache and see every model call as a costed span in a local trace viewer, **so that** I can iterate on prompts and graders without paying twice and can trace any case back to its model calls.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** see what a run actually cost next to what it would have cost, and how much came from the cache, **so that** replays never eat my budget and the measurements stay honest about spend.

<objective>
Add incurred_cost_usd to every attempt and to the response (D-29), make the runner cap and report spend on it with the notional total alongside, raise the runner read timeout to the D-28 worst case, and make graded summaries report incurred versus notional cost, the cache hit rate and the original model latency (D-19).

Purpose: the "hits report original latency and cost" and "summaries show incurred versus notional cost and the hit rate" parts of D-19 and LLM-05; RESEARCH Pitfall 3 and Pitfall 5.
Output: endpoint fields, runner accounting, grader and summary blocks, e2e assertions.
</objective>
