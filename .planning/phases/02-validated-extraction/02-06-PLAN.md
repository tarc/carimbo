# Phase 2 Plan 06: the offline grader understands validator outcomes and attempts

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** have every extraction target the full DANFE-visible invoice, pass deterministic validators and be repaired within a bounded budget, **so that** extracted invoices carry hard guarantees before anything is measured at scale.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** grade stored runs whose cases end as validation_failed or were repaired over several attempts, **so that** a run summary shows what the validators caught and what repair cost, without any model call.

<objective>
Teach `carimbo_evals.grader` and `summary` the eval contract version 2 vocabulary recorded in D-24 (CONTEXT D-14, D-15, D-18): the `validation_failed` status with its candidate invoice, `outcome.findings`, and the `attempts` list.

Purpose: offline grading stays the measurement surface (EVAL-02) while the pipeline gains validation and repair; the candidate of a validation_failed case is graded and counted as caught (input to EVAL-06 in Phase 5).
Output: grader and summary changes plus tests built on synthetic contract 2 records.
</objective>
