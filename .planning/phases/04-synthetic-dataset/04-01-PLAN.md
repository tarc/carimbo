# Phase 4 Plan 01: every validator-known tax code, ST and deferral, written, rendered and read back

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** evaluate against a seeded dataset of at least 150 varied and deliberately hard invoices whose ground truth is proven valid, **so that** measured accuracy reflects realistic difficulty instead of a few clean cases.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** have the generator write any CST or CSOSN the validators know, ST and deferral included, **so that** the dataset can carry mixed tax codes and the TAX_CODE_UNSUPPORTED warning path without breaking the skeleton.

<objective>
Extend the shared generator (spec.py, nfe_xml.py) additively so a CaseSpec item can use every CST and CSOSN the validators classify, with ICMS ST (CST 10, 30, 70) and deferral (CST 51), ST in the header totals and in vNF, and MEI (CRT 4) mapped to the Simples family. A new unit test proves each group against the official NF-e 4.00 XSD bundled in nfelib, the BrazilFiscalReport DANFE and the Python mapper. The skeleton profiles are untouched and their bytes do not move.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-04 (ST and deferral codes, mixed per-item codes of one regime family) and the carried-forward vNF formula; RESEARCH Pattern 3, Pitfall 7 and Pitfall 9 (XSD as a unit test of the writers, not a dataset gate; the stale nfe_xml.py docstring corrected). DATA-02 (several tax regimes) and DATA-04 (ground truth that the schema and validators accept).
Output: additive spec.py and nfe_xml.py changes, python/tests/test_tax_groups.py.

Ordering: this plan needs no new package, but every `uv run` syncs python/.venv from the lock on disk, so it runs after the supply-chain gate of plan 04-02 rather than beside it; otherwise it could install numpy and reportlab before the human approves them. It runs in parallel with plan 04-03 (no shared file).
</objective>
