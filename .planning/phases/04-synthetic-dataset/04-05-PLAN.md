# Phase 4 Plan 05: 90 base invoices across the regime and length strata, with tax-code and party traits

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** evaluate against a seeded dataset of at least 150 varied and deliberately hard invoices whose ground truth is proven valid, **so that** measured accuracy reflects realistic difficulty instead of a few clean cases.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** have every regime and length stratum filled with a known number of invoices, with ST and mixed codes, alphanumeric CNPJs, CPF recipients and MEI issuers, **so that** Phase 5 gets slices with a known n and every base already passes the gates.

<objective>
Widen the tracer quota table to the full base table of CONTEXT D-03: eight strata (regime x length) with exact counts, item bands, pinned edge counts and asserted page bands, tax-code traits (ST and deferral, mixed codes, MEI codes), party traits (alphanumeric issuer and recipient CNPJ, CPF recipient, MEI) and the per-case commercial details (IPI, freight and discount spread over items summing exactly to the header, installments). Regenerate data/dataset to 90 bases plus the tracer twin and keep every gate green.

Purpose: DATA-02 (single and multi-page, many line items, several tax regimes, at least 10 percent alphanumeric CNPJ) and DATA-05 (stratified split), per CONTEXT D-03, D-04 (ST and deferral, mixed per-item codes; per-item discount and freight summing exactly to the header, per the second D-04 note of 2026-10-29, a user decision following SEFAZ rejections 535 and 537), D-14 (split per base, stratified); RESEARCH Pattern 3, Pattern 6, Pitfall 2, Pitfall 7, Open Question 5 (MEI: 4 bases, tag mei). Long text and confusable values are plan 04-06; the image twins and the CI subset are plan 04-07.
Output: quota.py and dataset_spec.py widened, an installment helper in spec.py, test_dataset_spec.py, dataset checks in test_synthetic_only.py, regenerated data/dataset.
</objective>
