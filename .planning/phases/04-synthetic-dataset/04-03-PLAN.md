# Phase 4 Plan 03: image-only twins of a DANFE, rendered, degraded, wrapped and flagged

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** evaluate against a seeded dataset of at least 150 varied and deliberately hard invoices whose ground truth is proven valid, **so that** measured accuracy reflects realistic difficulty instead of a few clean cases.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** turn any text DANFE into deterministic image-only scans at known degradation levels, with a measured barcode flag, **so that** the dataset can test reading from pixels instead of a text layer.

<objective>
Create python/src/carimbo_datagen/scan.py: rasterize a text DANFE with pdfium, apply one degradation (none, rotation, blur, low DPI or a damaged barcode), encode grayscale JPEG and wrap the pages into an image-only PDF with reportlab, deterministically; decide barcode readability from the final bytes at a fixed decode DPI; prove there is no text layer. Freeze the parameters after looking at previews, before any twin is committed.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-07 (renderings), D-08 (two severities, tuned once by eye, recorded in code and in the manifest's generator block, where plan 04-04 writes scan_parameters() as the nested generator.scan object), D-09 (barcode_damaged plus a measured barcode_readable flag on every case), D-12 (realistic 200 DPI base scan); DATA-03. RESEARCH Pattern 1, Pattern 2, Code Examples 1 to 3, Pitfalls 4, 5, 10 and 11.
Output: scan.py and python/tests/test_scan.py (twins of the committed skeleton PDFs written to tmp_path only; nothing is committed under data/).
</objective>
