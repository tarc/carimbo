# Phase 4 Plan 04: tracer, one ST invoice and its rotated twin through the quota plan, the extended manifest and a byte-identical check in `just check` and CI

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** evaluate against a seeded dataset of at least 150 varied and deliberately hard invoices whose ground truth is proven valid, **so that** measured accuracy reflects realistic difficulty instead of a few clean cases.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** regenerate a versioned dataset directory from a quota table and a seed, and have `just check` and CI prove its bytes reproducible, **so that** the ground-truth gates (plan 04-09) and the full quota (plans 04-05 to 04-07) land on a pipeline that is already wired end to end.
