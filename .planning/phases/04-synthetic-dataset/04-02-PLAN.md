# Phase 4 Plan 02: the scan stack pinned behind a blocking supply-chain review

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** evaluate against a seeded dataset of at least 150 varied and deliberately hard invoices whose ground truth is proven valid, **so that** measured accuracy reflects realistic difficulty instead of a few clean cases.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** have the image-twin libraries pinned and reviewed before anything installs them, **so that** the degraded-scan pipeline rests on a supply chain I checked.

<objective>
Pin the libraries the image-only twins need as exact runtime dependencies, resolve the lock without installing, stop for a blocking human review of the new packages, then install the approved lock and prove the existing suite still passes.

Gate plan: the package-legitimacy protocol requires that nothing installs before the blocking review, so this plan cannot lead with a tracer. Its only work is the pin change and the reviewed lock (the same shape as the Phase 1 precedent, plan 01-01). The scan stage's tracer leads plan 04-03 and the phase tracer leads plan 04-04.

Purpose: CONTEXT carried-forward stack (Pillow 12.3.0, numpy 2.5.3, pypdfium2 5.13.0, reportlab 5.0.1, zxing-cpp 3.1.1) for DATA-03; RESEARCH "Package Legitimacy Audit" flagged numpy and reportlab (new) and the three already-locked packages as SUS by the seam (a release-date false positive for long-established packages), so a human checkpoint precedes the install.
Output: python/pyproject.toml and python/uv.lock.
</objective>
