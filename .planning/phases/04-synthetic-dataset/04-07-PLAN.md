# Phase 4 Plan 07: 64 image-only twins on eight curve bases, the CI subset and the manifest invariants

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** evaluate against a seeded dataset of at least 150 varied and deliberately hard invoices whose ground truth is proven valid, **so that** measured accuracy reflects realistic difficulty instead of a few clean cases.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** have each degradation measured on the same eight invoices, a fixed dev-only CI subset and tests that hold the manifest to its promises, **so that** Phase 5 can separate degradation from content and Phase 6 can gate PRs on a stable subset.

<objective>
Complete the dataset: designate the eight curve bases, render all eight twin renderings for each (64 twins, 154 cases), check the size budget before the image PDFs are committed once, widen the CI subset to the 20 cells of CONTEXT D-16, and add the tests that hold the committed files to DATA-02, DATA-03, DATA-05 and DATA-06.

Purpose: CONTEXT D-01 (at least 150), D-06 (paired twins with base_case), D-07 to D-10 (renderings, severities, barcode flag, about 40 percent image-only), D-11 and D-12 (commit everything within about 20 MB; realism before the cap), D-14 to D-16 (splits and CI subset), D-17 (manifest fields; its user-approved qualification of 2026-10-29 lets the phase-branch regenerations keep the label dataset-001 until it first merges to main, and forbids recording any eval run against dataset-001 before this plan commits the final bytes). RESEARCH Open Question 1 is settled here: eight curve bases with all eight renderings (n = 8 per slice, full degradation curves; Open Question 2 keeps n = 8 and leans on the paired twins). The twin XML byte copy of plan 04-04, the user-approved amendment of CONTEXT D-06/D-18 (2026-10-22), applies to all 64 twins.
Output: quota.py widened, a single-case builder in dataset.py if not present, test_dataset_scans.py, test_dataset_manifest.py, the full committed data/dataset.
</objective>
