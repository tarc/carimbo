# Phase 4 Plan 11: blur severe readable with effort, chosen by the user on the rendered twin (gap closure G-04-1)

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** evaluate against a seeded dataset of at least 150 varied and deliberately hard invoices whose ground truth is proven valid, **so that** measured accuracy reflects realistic difficulty instead of a few clean cases.

This plan's slice: **As a** developer, **I want to** have blur severe twins whose small digits a careful reader can still transcribe, **so that** the severe point of the degradation curve measures hard reading, not guaranteed misreading.

## Gap closed

- **G-04-1 (major, UAT test 1):** on nfe-0095 (blur severe twin of nfe-0001), read cold at native 220 DPI, item code D00137 reads D00132 and NCM 39191010 reads 39198890: sigma 2.4 turns small digits into misreads, not hard reads. Root cause: `DEGRADATION['blur']['severe'] = 2.4` (scan.py), frozen in plan 04-03 from 200 DPI previews and kept in pixels when plan 04-07 raised SCAN_DPI to 220. The user judged blur mild, both rotations, both low_dpi, image_clean and barcode_damaged fine (choice "A": only blur severe changes).

## Candidates (planner probe, 2026-10-29)

The planner re-rendered the eight blur-severe twins through `scan.render_twin` with the committed bases and the dataset's seed text: at 2.4 all eight equal the committed bytes, and at 1.2 nfe-0095 equals the committed nfe-0094 (blur mild of the same base; blur draws no random number, so the bytes depend only on sigma). None of the eight decodes at 1.6, 1.8 or 2.0. Totals of the eight PDFs: 1,208,192 bytes at 2.4 (committed), 1,338,690 at 2.0, 1,402,587 at 1.8, 1,495,574 at 1.6, 1,723,723 at 1.2, so every candidate keeps data/dataset under the cap. On a downscaled crop of nfe-0095's item rows the planner found the code and NCM legible at 1.8 and soft at 2.0; that impression is not the acceptance, the user's reading at native resolution is.

- **Proposed: 1.8** (1.5 times mild, about 0.21 mm at 220 DPI).
- **Fallback: 1.6**, if the user still misreads digits at 1.8.
- **Shown for calibration:** 2.0 (a harder alternative), 1.2 (mild, the floor) and 2.4 (current, the misread level).
