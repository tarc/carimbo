## The dc-004 miss (finding, not fixed)

dc-004 (the recipient is another company; expected reject, rule 2) was approved by the model. The record shows it cited both relevant values: `recipient.tax_id` 69979991000100 (from `get_invoice`) and `buyer_cnpj` CARIMBO0000177 (from `get_policy`), and its reason reads "Extraction successful, recipient tax ID matches policy, no validator warnings, and invoice total is within approval limit." The two values differ, so the comparison is wrong.

Consequences worth stating plainly:

- `citations valid` measures provenance (every evidence source was a tool the agent called), not whether the reasoning over the cited values is right. dc-004 passes it while being wrong.
- The deterministic guard only covers extraction status. A recipient mismatch is not caught by code.
- The score is n=1 per case, eight cases, one model: a demonstration of the loop and an honest miss, not an accuracy estimate.

Nothing was tuned: no change to prompts, rules, cases or the grader. Open item for a later phase, not decided here: a deterministic recipient check (compare `recipient.tax_id` to the policy's buyer CNPJ in code), or a comparison with a stronger model, or a prompt change.
