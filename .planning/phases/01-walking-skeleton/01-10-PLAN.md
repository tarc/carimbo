# Phase 1 Plan 10: LLM-06 spike — settle gateway shape and retry ownership on evidence

## Phase Goal

**As a** developer, **I want to** generate a few synthetic DANFEs, extract them live through the .NET eval endpoint and grade the stored results from Python, **so that** the whole measurement loop runs end to end on real code before any breadth is added.

<objective>
Run the LLM-06 spike that research specified, offline first and then live under a budget. Record the evidence in `docs/spikes/01-llm-gateway.md` (D-11) with sanitised fixtures, and stop at a decision checkpoint where the developer chooses the gateway's bottom adapter and the Phase 1 retry ownership.

Purpose: CONTEXT locks this choice to the spike, and only one adapter gets written, in plan 01-12. The developer's prior was the IChatClient adapter (research recommendation B). Offline research evidence leans toward the direct SDK, because the adapter rewrites schemas, sums cache tokens and collapses refusals. The human therefore decides after seeing live evidence.
Output: the `LlmSpike` tool, the spike document, three fixtures, and a recorded decision.
</objective>
