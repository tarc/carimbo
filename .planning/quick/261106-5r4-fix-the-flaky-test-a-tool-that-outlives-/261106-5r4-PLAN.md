<objective>
Remove the race behind the flaky `A_tool_that_outlives_the_call_timeout_is_a_timeout_on_the_real_session`: one `callTimeout` bounds both `McpAgentToolbox.ConnectAsync` (`AgentToolbox.cs:59`) and `Session.CallAsync` (`AgentToolbox.cs:140`), so a 500 ms budget meant for the call also has to cover a cold-start MCP handshake.

Purpose: `just check` must not fail on a test whose subject never ran. The failure text is `The Tools host did not finish listing the tools in time.`, raised at `AgentToolbox.cs:95`, reached from `AgentToolboxFaultTests.cs:30`. It failed 2 of 15 full `just check` runs. Reproduced while planning in a throwaway worktree at c97dee4: 24 concurrent test processes for 5 rounds failed 120 of 120 runs, all in this one test (119 with that text, 1 as `protocol_error` while listing). A candidate of exactly this shape in the same worktree gave 0 of 600, and every `devenv shell -- bash -c` command below ran against it, including the mutation proof (36 s, three caught). Raising 500 ms would only make the failure rarer and slow the test, so the connect step gets its own budget instead.

Output: `McpAgentToolbox` with an optional `connectTimeout`, the deflaked test, and two tests that pin the new parameter and its default.
</objective>
